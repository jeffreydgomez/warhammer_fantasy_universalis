# Mission Trigger Catalogue

CATALOGUE_VERSION: 2026-05-01
SOURCE: All current files in `missions/`.
SOURCE_SIZE: 51 mission files and more than 1600 mission trigger blocks.
PURPOSE: Catalogue the trigger vocabulary already used by the mod so new mission trees can reuse familiar patterns with intentional difficulty and flavor.

HOW_TO_USE:
- Start with the story beat, then choose one or two trigger families that prove that beat.
- Prefer triggers the player must pursue deliberately. Avoid conditions that are likely to be true on day one.
- Use clean, readable thresholds: 5, 10, 15, 25, 50, 75, 100, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9, 0.95, 1.0.
- Seen ranges are descriptive. Acceptable ranges are guidance for new mission design.
- Combine triggers when the flavor needs proof from multiple systems, but avoid making ordinary missions feel like checklists.

DIFFICULTY_BANDS:
- EARLY: completable with focused effort in about 50 years.
- MID: requires established regional strength or a developed internal system.
- LATE: expected around the End Times window, about 150 years in.
- ULTIMATE: final slot-3 mission difficulty, with about 35 reward value.
- PIE_IN_SKY: extremely hard aspirational mission, usually worth 3-4 ideas in reward strength.

TREE_PLACEMENT_SCALE:
- BEGINNING: rows 1-5. Use for setup, local consolidation, first reforms, small investments, and proof the player has started the story.
- MIDDLE: rows 6-12. Use for mature internal systems, regional strength, developed infrastructure, established diplomacy, and first decisive victories.
- END: rows 13-20. Use for capstones, full-region claims, world status, late tech, maximum institutions, and ultimate or pie-in-the-sky goals.
- VARIABLE: can appear anywhere, usually as a chaining, branch, or support condition. Pair it with a concrete objective when possible.

PLACEMENT_CLUSTERS:
- BEGINNING_CLUSTER: CAPITAL_AND_SYMBOLIC_PROVINCE, PROVINCE_QUALITY_TERRAIN_AND_BASE_STATS, BUILDINGS_AND_INFRASTRUCTURE, TREASURY_INCOME_AND_WEALTH, ARMY_SIZE_AND_MOBILIZATION, NAVY_SIZE_AND_FLEET_COMPOSITION, DIPLOMACY_ALLIANCES_TRUST_AND_OPINION, ESTATES_PRIVILEGES_AND_INTERNAL_FACTIONS, RELIGION_PIETY_AND_CONVERSION, ADVISORS_COURT_AND_ELITES, COMMANDERS_RULERS_AND_NAMED_PERSONNEL, RESOURCE_STOCKPILES_AND_POWER_BANKING, CORES_CLAIMS_COLONIES_AND_SETTLEMENT_STATE.
- MIDDLE_CLUSTER: DEVELOPMENT_GROWTH_AND_CITY_SCALE, PROSPERITY_DEVASTATION_AND_LOCAL_STABILITY, TRADE_NODE_DOMINANCE, CENTERS_OF_TRADE_AND_PORTS, TRADE_GOODS_AND_PRODUCTION, ARMY_QUALITY_TRADITION_AND_DRILL, UNIT_COMPOSITION, FORTS_DEFENSE_AND_FRONTIER_SECURITY, SUBJECTS_VASSALS_AND_LIBERTY_DESIRE, RIVALS_WARS_AND_HUMILIATION, STABILITY_LEGITIMACY_CROWN_LAND_AND_RULE, CULTURE_ACCEPTANCE_AND_IDENTITY, TECHNOLOGY_IDEAS_AND_INNOVATION, MONUMENTS_GREAT_PROJECTS_AND_RELICS.
- END_CLUSTER: DIRECT_OR_SUBJECT_LAND_CONTROL, AREA_REGION_OR_WORLD_CONTROL, PRIVATEERING_RAIDING_AND_COERCIVE_SEA_POWER, PRESTIGE_POWER_PROJECTION_AND_GREAT_POWER_STATUS, COLONIES_EXPLORATION_AND_OVERSEAS_REACH, GOVERNMENT_RANK_REFORMS_AND_SPECIAL_SYSTEMS, OPINION_OF_WORLD_OR_COUNTRY_SETS, COMPOUND_SCORE_OR_QUOTA, ESTABLISHED_INSTITUTION_OR_TIME_GATE.
- VARIABLE_CLUSTER: FLAGS_SCRIPTED_TRIGGERS_AND_CUSTOM_STATE, MODIFIERS_AND_ACTIVE_STATES, NEGATIVE_OR_ABSENCE_CHECKS, AI_OR_VISIBILITY_HELPERS.

PLACEMENT_NOTES:
- A trigger family can move earlier or later by changing scale. For example, one level 1 center of trade is a beginning/middle task, but several level 3 centers belong at the end.
- Cluster similar trigger families near each other in future trees so rows feel like chapters: early setup, middle consolidation, and late apotheosis.
- When a mission has multiple prerequisites, its trigger should usually represent a synthesis of those prerequisite themes.

GLOBAL_NO_START_COMPLETION_RULES:
- Add at least one active-effort gate to every new mission: growth, buildings, estate agenda, won war, institution, tech, idea group, developed province, upgraded center of trade, or a later prerequisite.
- Pure ownership of starting land, basic religion, starting estate setup, or starting ruler status should not be the only requirement.
- If using a simple status trigger such as `stability = 1`, pair it with a second trigger that proves action.

COMMON_WRAPPERS:
- `OR`: Used heavily for alternate routes, especially direct ownership vs subject ownership, alliances vs subject control, or multiple valid targets.
- `AND`: Used for tightly bound proof, such as province ownership plus building plus prosperity.
- `NOT`: Used for "less than" style gates, especially `NOT = { liberty_desire = X }`, `NOT = { great_power_rank = 9 }`, or undeveloped province checks.
- `calc_true_if`: Use when several options can satisfy a mission and only some are required. Flavor: assembling a coalition, collecting relics, meeting a quota from scattered targets.
- `custom_trigger_tooltip`: Use when the engine syntax would be unreadable. Keep the underlying trigger exact and the tooltip plain.

GLOBAL_ALTERNATE_TRIGGER_RULES:
- Whenever a mission uses `army_professionalism = N`, allow `war_modern_army` as an alternate proof of modernization:
```
OR = {
	army_professionalism = N
	has_country_modifier = war_modern_army
}
```
- Whenever a mission uses `innovativeness = N`, allow `war_modern_science` as an alternate proof of modernization:
```
OR = {
	innovativeness = N
	has_country_modifier = war_modern_science
}
```
- Whenever a mission uses `has_building = X`, include buildings in the same replacement line when a higher or lower tier represents the same investment. For example, a barracks requirement should also accept training fields.
- Whenever a mission uses manufactories as a trigger, accept any manufactory building unless the story specifically requires one industry or trade good.

COMMON_SCOPES:
- `capital_scope`: Capital city proof, state formation, court centralization, sacred seat, primary port.
- `any_owned_province`: One qualifying owned province. Good for "establish a foothold" missions.
- `all_owned_province`: Nationwide standard. Use carefully; it can be tedious.
- `num_of_owned_provinces_with`: Count owned provinces matching filters. This is the standard count trigger.
- `num_of_provinces_owned_or_owned_by_subjects_with`: Count direct and subject lands. Use for hegemony and imperial reach.
- `num_of_provinces_owned_or_owned_by_non_sovereign_subjects_with`: Count direct and integrated-style subjects, excluding independent allies. Use for overlord systems.
- `any_country` / `all_country`: Diplomatic, rival, war-history, and global target scans.
- `any_subject_country` / `all_subject_country`: Subject management, empire structure, tribute, and vassal loyalty.
- `home_trade_node` / `all_trade_node`: Trade-control checks.

COVERAGE_NOTE:
- The mission corpus also uses many specific province ids, tags, region names, modifier names, estate names, trade good names, and mechanic names. This catalogue groups those as trigger families rather than listing every id.
- High-frequency implementation keys such as `value`, `who`, `type`, `estate`, `owner`, `country`, `share`, `limit`, and `tooltip` are parameters of the trigger families below, not separate mission concepts.

TRIGGER_TYPE: DIRECT_OR_SUBJECT_LAND_CONTROL
TREE_PLACEMENT: SCALING, but usually MIDDLE_TO_END. Small footholds can appear at the beginning; full areas, regions, and imperial claims belong at the end.
COMMON_KEYS:
- `owned_by = ROOT`
- `owned_by_subject_of = ROOT`
- `controlled_by = ROOT`
- `controlled_by = FROM`
- `owns_core_province = PROVINCE_ID`
- `num_of_owned_provinces_with = { value = N ... }`
- `num_of_provinces_owned_or_owned_by_subjects_with = { value = N ... }`
SEEN_NUMERIC_RANGES:
- Province count values appear from 1 upward, usually 2-25 for focused missions and much higher for empire checks.
- `num_of_cities` appears from 10 to 75.
ACCEPTABLE_RANGES:
- EARLY: 2-5 named provinces, one small area, or `num_of_cities = 10`.
- MID: 6-12 provinces, several ports, one important area, or `num_of_cities = 20-25`.
- LATE: 15-30 provinces, a full region path, or `num_of_cities = 40-50`.
- ULTIMATE: whole homeland, full region, or `num_of_cities = 75`.
- PIE_IN_SKY: multiple regions, continent-scale control, or a full imperial map claim.
FLAVOR_IMPLICATIONS:
- Direct ownership says settlement, consolidation, incorporation, colonization, or purification.
- Subject ownership says tribute, hegemony, puppet rule, protectorates, or indirect empire.
- Temporary control says wartime seizure, shock campaign, humiliation, or raiding success.
DESIGN_NOTES:
- Use direct ownership for internal state-building stories.
- Use direct-or-subject ownership when the mission should respect vassal play.
- Avoid too many pure conquest triggers. Pair land goals with buildings, trade, culture, religion, or estates.

TRIGGER_TYPE: AREA_REGION_OR_WORLD_CONTROL
TREE_PLACEMENT: END. Small area footholds can sit in the middle; full regions, continents, and world reach should be late, ultimate, or pie-in-the-sky.
COMMON_KEYS:
- Province scopes filtered by `area`, `region`, `superregion`, `continent`, `province_id`, or scripted province groups.
- `type = all` inside province-count triggers.
- `num_of_continents = N`
SEEN_NUMERIC_RANGES:
- `num_of_continents = 2-7`.
- Full-area or full-region checks usually use `type = all` instead of a numeric threshold.
ACCEPTABLE_RANGES:
- EARLY: one strategic area or foothold on a second continent.
- MID: one full region edge, 2-3 continents, or several named strategic provinces.
- LATE: major region control, 4-5 continents, or a chain of distant outposts.
- ULTIMATE: homeland plus primary rival region, or 5-7 continents.
- PIE_IN_SKY: world-spanning presence, multiple superregions, or total control of a legendary geography.
FLAVOR_IMPLICATIONS:
- Area checks imply local consolidation and practical control.
- Region checks imply historic unification or geopolitical dominance.
- Continent checks imply exploration, diaspora, trade empire, or apocalyptic reach.
DESIGN_NOTES:
- If geography is only a backdrop, prefer a smaller count plus a second system trigger.
- Full-region checks should usually sit in late, ultimate, or pie-in-sky positions.

TRIGGER_TYPE: DEVELOPMENT_GROWTH_AND_CITY_SCALE
TREE_PLACEMENT: BEGINNING_TO_MIDDLE. Use small growth and first developed cities early; reserve 40-60 dev, 800+ total dev, and huge growth for the end.
COMMON_KEYS:
- `development = N`
- `total_development = N`
- `total_own_and_non_tributary_subject_development = N`
- `grown_by_development = N`
- `grown_by_states = N`
- `development_in_provinces = N`
- `num_of_cities = N`
SEEN_NUMERIC_RANGES:
- Province `development = 4-60`.
- `total_development = 50-2500`.
- `grown_by_development = 50, 300, 900`.
- `grown_by_states = 5`.
- `num_of_cities = 10-75`.
ACCEPTABLE_RANGES:
- EARLY: province dev 10-20, total dev 50-150, grow by 50, or 10 cities.
- MID: province dev 25-35, total dev 200-400, grow by 150-300, or 20-25 cities.
- LATE: province dev 40-50, total dev 800-1000, grow by 500-900, or 40-50 cities.
- ULTIMATE: province dev 60, total dev 1500-2500, or 75 cities.
- PIE_IN_SKY: multiple 50+ dev provinces, 2500+ total dev, or massive growth compared to start.
FLAVOR_IMPLICATIONS:
- Single-province development means a capital, fortress city, shrine city, port, forge, or academy.
- Total development means state capacity and population.
- Growth from start means reform, settlement, recovery, industrialization, or migration.
DESIGN_NOTES:
- Growth triggers are excellent anti-start-completion gates.
- Use province dev for iconic places. Use total dev for realm-wide transformation.

TRIGGER_TYPE: BUILDINGS_AND_INFRASTRUCTURE
TREE_PLACEMENT: BEGINNING_TO_MIDDLE. Basic buildings make strong opening objectives; upgraded networks, universities, manufactories, and heavy infrastructure belong later.
COMMON_KEYS:
- `has_building = temple`
- `has_building = cathedral`
- `has_building = marketplace`
- `has_building = trade_depot`
- `has_building = stock_exchange`
- `has_building = workshop`
- `has_building = counting_house`
- `has_building = courthouse`
- `has_building = town_hall`
- `has_building = barracks`
- `has_building = training_fields`
- `has_building = regimental_camp`
- `has_building = conscription_center`
- `has_building = dock`
- `has_building = drydock`
- `has_building = shipyard`
- `has_building = grand_shipyard`
- `has_building = university`
- `has_building = farm_estate`
- `has_building = mills`
- `has_building = plantations`
- `has_building = textile`
- `has_building = tradecompany`
- `has_building = weapons`
- `has_building = wharf`
- `has_building = furnace`
- `num_of_buildings_in_province = N`
- `num_of_times_expanded_infrastructure = N`
BUILDING_TECH_UNLOCK_GROUPS:
- BEGINNING_GAME_UNLOCKS: Use for row 1-5 setup missions, unless asking for a large count. These are available at or very near the main start: `fort_15th` at MIL tech 1, `temple` at ADM tech 4, and `marketplace` at DIP tech 4.
- EARLY_GAME_UNLOCKS: Use for early tree missions or the first half of a tree. These unlock from tech 5-8: `coastal_defence` at DIP tech 5; `farm_estate`, `ramparts`, `workshop`, `dock`, and `barracks` at tech 6; `impressment_offices` at DIP tech 7; `courthouse`, `shipyard`, and `regimental_camp` at tech 8.
- MID_GAME_UNLOCKS: Use for middle-tree consolidation and missions intended to be reachable by the End Times pacing window. These unlock from tech 11-16: `textile`, `wharf`, and `weapons` at tech 11; `state_house` and `naval_battery` at tech 12; `plantations`, `tradecompany`, and `fort_16th` at tech 14; `soldier_households` at tech 15; `mills` and `training_fields` at tech 16.
- LATE_END_UNLOCKS: Use for late, ultimate, or pie-in-the-sky missions. These unlock at tech 17+: `university` and `trade_depot` at tech 17; `cathedral`, `drydock`, and `fort_17th` at tech 19; `furnace` at tech 21; `town_hall`, `stock_exchange`, and `conscription_center` at tech 22; `counting_house`, `grand_shipyard`, and `fort_18th` at tech 24.
- NATIVE_BUILDING_UNLOCKS: `native_earthwork`, `native_palisade`, `native_fortified_house`, `native_three_sisters_field`, `native_irrigation`, `native_storehouse`, `native_longhouse`, `native_sweat_lodge`, `native_great_trail`, and `native_ceremonial_fire_pit` unlock at ADM tech 0. Use only for native-specific mission trees.
BUILDING_TECH_DESIGN_NOTES:
- Building unlock timing is a floor, not the whole difficulty. A single `temple` can be a beginning mission; 10 temples across a region is a middle or late mission.
- Do not require LATE_END_UNLOCKS in the first half of a normal tree unless the mission is intentionally time-gated.
- `manufactory` is the parent building definition; for mission triggers, prefer the specific manufactory buildings listed above.
BUILDING_REPLACEMENT_OR_GROUPS:
- TEMPLE_LINE: `temple`, `cathedral`.
- TRADE_LINE: `marketplace`, `trade_depot`, `stock_exchange`.
- PRODUCTION_LINE: `workshop`, `counting_house`.
- GOVERNMENT_LINE: `courthouse`, `town_hall`.
- ARMY_MANPOWER_LINE: `barracks`, `training_fields`.
- ARMY_FORCE_LIMIT_LINE: `regimental_camp`, `conscription_center`.
- DOCK_LINE: `dock`, `drydock`.
- SHIPYARD_LINE: `shipyard`, `grand_shipyard`.
- FORT_LINE: `fort_15th`, `fort_16th`, `fort_17th`, `fort_18th`.
- MANUFACTORY_LINE: `mills`, `farm_estate`, `tradecompany`, `plantations`, `textile`, `weapons`, `wharf`, `furnace`.
BUILDING_REPLACEMENT_EXAMPLE:
```
num_of_provinces_owned_or_owned_by_subjects_with = {
	value = 10
	OR = {
		has_building = barracks
		has_building = training_fields
	}
}
```
MANUFACTORY_EXAMPLE:
```
num_of_owned_provinces_with = {
	region = dragon_isles_region
	OR = {
		has_building = mills
		has_building = farm_estate
		has_building = tradecompany
		has_building = plantations
		has_building = textile
		has_building = weapons
		has_building = wharf
		has_building = furnace
	}
	value = 1
}
```
SEEN_NUMERIC_RANGES:
- `num_of_buildings_in_province = 1-7`.
- `num_of_times_expanded_infrastructure = 1-4`.
- Building count missions commonly use 1-10 qualifying provinces.
ACCEPTABLE_RANGES:
- EARLY: 1 important building, or 2-4 provinces with a basic building.
- MID: 5-8 provinces with a building line, one province with 3-5 buildings, or infrastructure expanded once.
- LATE: 10-15 provinces, upgraded building lines, universities, manufactories, or infrastructure 2-3 times.
- ULTIMATE: many upgraded buildings across the homeland, or infrastructure 4 times in a symbolic province.
- PIE_IN_SKY: complete built-out economic, military, or sacred network across a whole region.
FLAVOR_IMPLICATIONS:
- Temples and cathedrals: piety, cult reform, legitimacy, sacred order.
- Marketplaces and trade buildings: merchant houses, ports, tolls, caravan systems.
- Workshops and manufactories: production, industry, mines, workshops, logistics.
- Courthouses and town halls: bureaucracy, pacification, state law, anti-corruption.
- Barracks and camps: militarization, levies, warrior caste, frontier muster.
- Docks and shipyards: fleet building, naval projection, piracy, exploration.
- Universities: scholarship, magical research, legal reform, enlightened rule.
DESIGN_NOTES:
- Building triggers are good for mission trees that should not be pure conquest.
- Use the building type as the narrative noun of the mission.
- Use replacement OR groups for ordinary building-line proof. Only require one specific tier when the mission is intentionally about that exact building or a late tech gate.
- Use the full manufactory OR group for generic industry or production missions. Only narrow to a specific manufactory when the mission is about a specific trade good or local industry.

TRIGGER_TYPE: CAPITAL_AND_SYMBOLIC_PROVINCE
TREE_PLACEMENT: BEGINNING_TO_MIDDLE. Early missions can found or reclaim the place; end missions can perfect it into a 50-60 dev capital or fully upgraded sacred site.
COMMON_KEYS:
- `capital_scope = { ... }`
- Named province id scopes.
- `owns = PROVINCE_ID`
- `province_id = PROVINCE_ID`
- Province flags and scripted province checks.
SEEN_NUMERIC_RANGES:
- Capital development appears around 30-35 in several files, with province development up to 60 elsewhere.
- Center of trade levels on symbolic provinces appear at 1-3.
ACCEPTABLE_RANGES:
- EARLY: own the place and add one building or 10-15 development.
- MID: 25-35 development, center of trade level 2, prosperity, or several buildings.
- LATE: 40-50 development, center of trade level 3, monument tier 2-3, or full defensive/economic buildout.
- ULTIMATE: 60 development or a fully upgraded sacred capital.
- PIE_IN_SKY: turn a mythic capital into an unmatched city with dev, buildings, monument, trade, and prosperity.
FLAVOR_IMPLICATIONS:
- Capital scope implies centralization, court reform, royal legitimacy, cult seat, or reborn metropolis.
- Named province scope implies reclaiming a landmark, awakening a site, fortifying a pass, or rebuilding ruins.
DESIGN_NOTES:
- Capital missions work best when they unlock or follow administrative, estate, or legitimacy story beats.

TRIGGER_TYPE: PROVINCE_QUALITY_TERRAIN_AND_BASE_STATS
TREE_PLACEMENT: BEGINNING_TO_MIDDLE. Use terrain, city status, and first base-stat targets early; use extreme specialization only near the end.
COMMON_KEYS:
- `has_terrain = X`
- `is_city = yes`
- `base_tax = N`
- `base_production = N`
- `base_manpower = N`
- `province_trade_power = N`
- `development = N`
SEEN_NUMERIC_RANGES:
- `base_production = 5-15`.
- `base_manpower = 2-10`.
- `province_trade_power = 20-50`.
- Raw `development = 4-60`.
ACCEPTABLE_RANGES:
- EARLY: base stat 5, province trade power 20, or an owned city in the right terrain.
- MID: base stat 7-10, province trade power 30-40, or terrain plus building/prosperity.
- LATE: base stat 15, province trade power 50, or a fully specialized province.
- ULTIMATE: iconic province with high base stats, terrain significance, buildings, and monument or fort.
- PIE_IN_SKY: multiple iconic provinces remade into perfect economic, military, or sacred centers.
FLAVOR_IMPLICATIONS:
- Terrain says mountain defense, desert survival, jungle mastery, steppe mobility, island identity, or river control.
- Base tax says administration, population, noble estates, or urban wealth.
- Base production says mines, farms, workshops, plantations, or extraction.
- Base manpower says recruitment grounds, warrior culture, levies, or population depth.
- Province trade power says harbor dominance, market city status, choke point, or caravan hub.
DESIGN_NOTES:
- Province quality triggers are best when the specific place matters to the story.
- Avoid raw base-stat chores unless paired with a strong province identity.

TRIGGER_TYPE: PROSPERITY_DEVASTATION_AND_LOCAL_STABILITY
TREE_PLACEMENT: MIDDLE. Prosperity needs time to develop; whole-homeland peace and recovery belongs at the end.
COMMON_KEYS:
- `is_prosperous = yes`
- `devastation = N`
- `prosperity = N`
- `has_unrest = no`
- `revolt_risk = N`
- `is_in_capital_area = yes`
SEEN_NUMERIC_RANGES:
- Prosperity is usually boolean through `is_prosperous`.
- Devastation and unrest checks are rarer than prosperity.
ACCEPTABLE_RANGES:
- EARLY: one prosperous state or no unrest in a core province.
- MID: several prosperous states or no devastation in a recovered region.
- LATE: whole homeland prosperous, pacified, and built.
- ULTIMATE: empire-wide peace after conquest or disaster.
FLAVOR_IMPLICATIONS:
- Prosperity says peace, reconstruction, good rule, irrigation, trade safety, or sacred harmony.
- Low unrest says pacification, integration, fear, legitimacy, or cultural settlement.
DESIGN_NOTES:
- Pair prosperity with buildings or development so the player has active agency.

TRIGGER_TYPE: TRADE_NODE_DOMINANCE
TREE_PLACEMENT: MIDDLE_TO_END. Local 10-20 percent trade share can be early/middle; home-node dominance, monopolies, and multiple nodes should be late.
COMMON_KEYS:
- `home_trade_node = { trade_share = { country = ROOT share = N } }`
- `trade_share = { country = ROOT share = N }`
- `is_strongest_trade_power = ROOT`
- `trading_part = N`
- `transfers_trade_power_to = ROOT`
- `province_trade_power = N`
- `mercantilism = N`
- `production_leader = TRADE_GOOD`
- `trading_bonus = TRADE_GOOD`
SEEN_NUMERIC_RANGES:
- Trade share values appear around 1, 10, 15, 20, 50, 51, 65, 75, 90.
- `trading_part` appears as a threshold-style trade participation check.
- `province_trade_power = 20-50`.
- `mercantilism = 15-25`.
ACCEPTABLE_RANGES:
- EARLY: 10-20 percent share, strongest local node, or one trade-transfer partner.
- MID: 50-65 percent share, mercantilism 15-20, home node dominance, or trading bonus for a key good.
- LATE: 75-90 percent share, mercantilism 25, multiple nodes, or production leadership.
- ULTIMATE: dominate home node plus strategic downstream/upstream nodes.
- PIE_IN_SKY: near-monopoly over a full trade route or several signature goods.
FLAVOR_IMPLICATIONS:
- Trade share means merchant dominance, shipping lanes, caravan power, tariffs, or port hierarchy.
- Trading bonus or production leader means resource monopoly and economic identity.
- Transfer trade power means coerced commerce, protectorates, treaties, or tributary markets.
DESIGN_NOTES:
- Trade triggers make conquest feel economic rather than territorial.
- Use `home_trade_node` for domestic consolidation and all-node scopes for maritime empires.

TRIGGER_TYPE: CENTERS_OF_TRADE_AND_PORTS
TREE_PLACEMENT: MIDDLE_TO_END. Level 1 centers and first ports can appear early; level 2-3 centers and port chains should cluster in the middle and end.
COMMON_KEYS:
- `province_has_center_of_trade_of_level = N`
- `has_port = yes`
- `province_trade_power = N`
- `num_of_owned_provinces_with = { has_port = yes value = N }`
- `has_building = marketplace`
- `has_building = trade_depot`
- `has_building = stock_exchange`
SEEN_NUMERIC_RANGES:
- Center of trade level appears at 1, 2, and 3.
- Port counts commonly sit in the 2-10 range, with larger counts for naval empires.
ACCEPTABLE_RANGES:
- EARLY: one level 1 center of trade or 2-3 ports.
- MID: one or more level 2 centers, or 5-8 ports with trade/naval buildings.
- LATE: level 3 centers in key locations, or 10+ ports.
- ULTIMATE: several level 3 centers along a strategic route.
- PIE_IN_SKY: all key ports upgraded and defended across a sea or ocean system.
FLAVOR_IMPLICATIONS:
- Centers of trade imply entrepots, caravan hubs, city markets, naval choke points, or tribute harbors.
- Ports imply sea-lanes, migration, corsairs, exploration, or invasion logistics.
DESIGN_NOTES:
- Center of trade levels are strong mid-to-late gates because they require investment and institution timing.

TRIGGER_TYPE: TRADE_GOODS_AND_PRODUCTION
TREE_PLACEMENT: MIDDLE_TO_END. Signature goods can be introduced early, but production leadership and monopolies are late-tree objectives.
COMMON_KEYS:
- `trade_goods = X`
- `trade_goods_produced_amount = N`
- `base_production = N`
- `production_leader = X`
- `trading_bonus = X`
SEEN_NUMERIC_RANGES:
- `base_production = 5-15`.
- Goods-produced amount appears frequently as a quantity gate.
ACCEPTABLE_RANGES:
- EARLY: one province producing the signature good, or base production 5-6.
- MID: several provinces, base production 7-10, or a moderate goods-produced target.
- LATE: base production 15, production leader, or trading bonus.
- ULTIMATE: dominate the signature resource at regional scale.
- PIE_IN_SKY: effective monopoly on a faction-defining resource.
FLAVOR_IMPLICATIONS:
- Goods checks imply mines, food supply, beasts, timber, weapons, gems, magic materials, or luxury markets.

TRIGGER_TYPE: GOVERNMENT_RANK_REFORMS_AND_SPECIAL_SYSTEMS

DESIGN_RECIPES:
- Internal reform mission: estate agenda 1-2, stability 1-2, crown land 50, and one courthouse/town hall or government reform.
- Economic development mission: grow by 50-300 development, build workshops/manufactories, reach monthly income 25-50, or dominate a trade good.
- Sacred consolidation mission: religious unity 0.95-1.0, own holy site, upgrade monument, and keep clergy/magic estate loyal.
- Military modernization mission: army size percentage 0.9-1.0, professionalism 0.25-0.50, drilled regiments 10-25, and a military building network.
- Naval projection mission: navy size 40-100, flagship, ports 5-10, light/heavy ship threshold, and trade node share 50+.
- Diplomatic league mission: 2-4 allies, opinion 100-200, trust 75-100, and one shared rival or war-history proof.
- Subject empire mission: 2-7 subjects, liberty desire below 50/25/10, subject-owned province counts, and trade transfer/favors.
- Legendary capstone mission: combine 3-4 systems, such as full region control, capital 50-60 development, monument tier 3, great-power rank 1-2, and a defining enemy defeated.

ANTI_PATTERNS:
- Do not use a single day-one status as the whole trigger.
- Do not use high treasury alone unless waiting is intended.
- Do not use full-region conquest repeatedly in the same tree.
- Do not use high estate influence as a reward-like goal unless the story wants the estate empowered.
- Do not use tech gates early unless the mission is explicitly a late timeline marker.
- Do not make every mission require conquest; use buildings, estates, trade, diplomacy, development, religion, and military quality to tell progress.
