# Enclave flag sources and artwork decisions

Research date: 27 September 2026. Warhammer lore and Total War sources were searched first for every proposed faction. The [search log](search_log.json) records the research batches. Sources describe the underlying faction unless a row explicitly says they establish its heraldry.

**Final artwork:** 100 individually generated illustrations, converted to 128×128, 24-bit uncompressed TGA for EU4. Two use explicit heraldry descriptions (Aucassin and Long Drong); the remaining 98 are original interpretations. None is presented as an extracted Total War asset or a scanned canonical banner. No flag-image downloads were successfully incorporated.

Several promising sources identify existing banners, notably Dieter Helsnicht, Cluster Eye, Wargrove of Woe, Clan Septik, Clan Kreepus, and Clan Feesik. Direct page/image retrieval was blocked by robots restrictions or browser challenges at the first lore hosts and attempted alternate galleries. The accessible text and lore informed new illustrations; unverified designs are labeled original. No challenge or access restriction was bypassed.

The second pass examined [FOTW's fictional flags](https://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/fic.html), [pirate-style fictional flags](https://www.crwflags.com/fotw/Flags/fic-pira.html), [Corsair](https://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/fic_cors.html), and [fictional forest flag designs](https://www.reddit.com/r/vexillology/comments/re8j7t). These offered heraldic layout references, but no outside fantasy nation's flag was adopted for an unrelated enclave.

All images used the built-in imagegen tool, with each flag generated separately. Aucassin received one additional [background correction](Y10-revision.json) to restore its white field. Initial prompts are in [prompts.json](prompts.json); generated source locations are in [generated.jsonl](generated.jsonl). Only square resizing, RGB conversion, and TGA encoding were performed afterward. The retained project masters are in [Flag Artwork](../Flag%20Artwork).
