# Enclave description sources and adaptation notes

The province modifier and Root Out decision descriptions in `localisation/war_enclave_provinces_l_english.yml` were revised on 2026-09-27. Each modifier now identifies its leader, local refuge, and activities. Each decision repeats that overview, adds a specific reason to confront the faction, and retains the notice of immediate separatist occupation.

The approved [catalogue](../Minor%20Faction%20Research.md), the faction `basis` fields in [countries.json](countries.json), and the [leader register](Leaders.md) remain the source record for all 100 entries. Original leaders, exact enclave placements, and invented local activities are mod fiction. They are written as in-world facts in game, without presenting them here as published Warhammer history. The descriptions add no invented historical dates or named battles. Regional religion and politics follow [Lore Reference.txt](../Examples/Lore%20Reference.txt).

## Chronology and local canon

- **Existing religion mappings (2026-09-28):** E02 and E39 now use Gork n' Mork. Forest Goblins retain their spider traditions in the descriptions, consistent with their [worship of the Spider alongside Gork and Mork](https://whfb.lexicanum.com/wiki/Spider_God). E20, E23, E27, E47, E48, E70, and E77 use the mod's existing Animist faith for local customs and spirit beliefs. E30 also uses Animist to represent the Golden Magus' [command of elemental spirits](https://whfb.lexicanum.com/wiki/Golden_Magus). These are gameplay mappings, not new claims about canonical religious affiliations. The existing Animist startup description already covers spirits of places and natural forces. No separate religion is retained for these traditions.

- **Zacharias:** his established position has already been brought forward by the approved faction list. His later invasion of Wissenland is omitted rather than reassigned to an invented earlier year.
- **Coeddil:** the text begins with him imprisoned. It does not import his End Times release into the starting world-state.
- **Alhazred, Hellbeard, and Brachnar:** the approved living presence or surviving refuge is retained. This does not establish an independently verified canonical 2502 polity in the assigned province.
- **Vashnaar:** Gorepeak is a gathering ground. His recorded **2521** invasion of Lustria is not described as past history.
- **Bregonne:** the approved early Axmin settlement is retained without presenting the source's **2510** foundation as a past event at the 2502 start. See [Bregonne](https://whfb.lexicanum.com/wiki/Bregonne).
- **Rikzik Seepage:** his use as a leader is the existing adaptation. Later End Times campaigns are omitted.
- **Painted Skin:** Tzeentch is the mod's chosen patron, not a resolution of the published cult's ambiguous allegiance.
- **Festerlingus and Gristlecrack:** the former's local Pestilens connection and the latter's flesh-workshops and Moulder trade are approved mod premises. These are not claims that their published clan histories establish those exact specializations.
- **Original factions:** concrete local details develop their approved premises; invented leaders remain labeled in the leader register. No fabricated source, historic year, or canonical campaign is attached to them.
