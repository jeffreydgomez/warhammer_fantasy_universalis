Ordinary mission rewards total 179.5 WV, plus permanent explorer recruitment (unpriced in the guide).
The ultimate copies Westerland's 51% share in 15 trade nodes and its four modifiers, worth 85 WV.
Formation and unity/diversity rewards are separate from mission budgets.
Four mission choice events each grant exactly 5 WV regardless of option.
Native mission IDs and permanent modifiers persist across change_tag. All five columns use Pigbarter tag history, so the tree persists after forming Swinetrade.
Opening missions require capital investment, new docks, a completed agenda, or trusted foreign contacts.
State-prosperity concepts use province quotas, matching existing local mission triggers.
Regional conquest uses direct or loyal-subject land; alliances require independence, 60 trust, 100 opinion and substantial local holdings.

All 24 former one-off rewards are now permanent country modifiers.
Darklands settlement covers all 152 provinces in the Darklands superregion, allowing mixed direct, loyal-subject and trusted-allied ownership.
