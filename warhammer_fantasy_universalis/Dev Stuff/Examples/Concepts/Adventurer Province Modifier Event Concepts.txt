WARHAMMER FANTASY UNIVERSALIS
ADVENTURER PROVINCE MODIFIERS - EVENT CONCEPT DOCUMENT
Design draft | 2502 IC opening | No game code

SCOPE AND READING GUIDE

This document covers the approved 80-entry roster, grouped by the 22 populated
Warhammer superregions in map/superregion.txt. Each entry has a fame rating,
a specific starting province and owner, one small passive province bonus, a
relationship profile, and exactly five event concepts: three Friendly/Neutral
and two Enemy. Total: 400 concepts, 240 Friendly/Neutral and 160 Enemy.

Pairs and named companies remain one traveling modifier. The five concepts are
alternative encounters across successive visits, not five stages that all happen
before departure. Every resolved encounter moves the adventurer, regardless of
the option chosen. None of these new episodes is asserted to be a documented
canonical achievement: they are original mod stories inspired by character lore.

Fame is the previous editorial fan-recognition score, not an in-world statistic:
5 iconic; 4 well known; 3 familiar to engaged fans; 2 specialist; 1 obscure.
Fame affects flavor prominence, not passive bonus strength. Supporting members
can be less famous than the lead; mixed ratings are retained where previously given.

STARTING PLACEMENTS AND EXCEPTIONS

The roster uses 79 different country tags and one unowned location. All 22
superregions have two to five allocated entries, including reserved arrivals.
The exact province anchors below refine the previously approved country-level
placements. Capitals are used where they belong to the required superregion;
otherwise a verified owned province in that superregion is used. Country names,
province names, ownership, and superregion membership come from the local mod,
including its localisation/replace files. These anchors are design locations,
not a claim that canonical books put each character there in 2502.

- Valten: reserve his Sudenland slot for a later adult emergence. Ballenhof is a
  proposed arrival site, NOT a claim that canonical Lachenbad is Ballenhof. No
  active Valten modifier, passive benefit, or Valten event exists at game start.
- Blue Scribes: Helin (4062) is unowned. Reserve the location without an active
  owner-facing event or economic bonus. Activate only when it obtains an owner,
  or introduce the Scribes through a separately agreed owned-province arrival.
  Do not silently give this province to a country just to make the system work.
- Oxyotl: the approved start needs a deliberate timeline departure: a temporary
  rift excursion or earlier return from the Realm of Chaos. Until that premise
  is accepted for implementation, keep his slot dormant. His five concepts
  apply after arrival and do not create teleportation exceptions to movement.
- Morgan Bernhardt already rules A94 in country history. Before activation as an
  independent traveler, reconcile that role; do not duplicate him as both an
  autonomous ruler and an unrelated wandering company. His proposed visit is
  in Pigbarter's Darklands holding, not automatically Pigbarter's capital.
- Kruber's early guard career, Gelt's eastern studies, and several other foreign
  assignments are explicit mod inventions. No Vermintide team is assembled and
  no later Grail Knight, vampire, or necromancer career is silently backdated.
- Ulrika uses her human identity. A later vampire transformation would require
  revising her relationship profile and text, not merely leaving human events.
- Bugman's Ranger entry assumes the brewery catastrophe has already occurred.
  Hengus starts before removal from Albion, unless a return journey is chosen.
- Kroak travels as a guarded sacred relic procession. Chakax accompanies an
  authorized sacred escort. Court champions travel on temporary assignments;
  residence changes do not transfer their personal loyalty to the host ruler.
- The current files assign province 4751, localized as Wastes of Khuresh, to the
  Southern Chaos Wastes. This document follows the file's membership rather
  than silently correcting geography based on the province name.

RELATIONSHIP CLASSIFICATION

At each encounter, classify the current owner afresh. Consider the country's
primary/accepted cultures, state religion, explicit alignment, personal patrons,
active contracts, and what the government is doing. Provincial culture alone
must not override the owner: a human population under a necromantic ruler can
receive an Enemy event about a witch hunter confronting its government.

Precedence: (1) direct personal target, betrayal, persecution, or sacred violation;
(2) hard ideological conflict explicitly named in the entry; (3) reliable patron,
contract, or common defense; (4) otherwise neutral. An existing short truce or
accepted mercenary contract can override ordinary rivalry, but never silently
turn a Daemon into a loyal Sigmarite servant or compel a Slayer to aid Chaos.
Use the entry's exceptions before applying broad racial assumptions.

Friendly/Neutral is one event category. Its three concepts portray cooperation,
incidental benefit, or a wary opportunity. Enemy is the other category; its two
concepts portray the adventurer opposing the current government. The owner can
mitigate, negotiate, obstruct, or accept consequences. 'Enemy' does not mean the
adventurer has become morally evil: a hero freeing a tyrant's prisoners harms
that tyrant. 'Friendly' can describe a villain benefiting an evil patron.

Mercenaries need contract-based exceptions. Independent vampires need personal
rather than blanket vampire allegiance. Asur civil-war sides, legally tolerated
undead governments, the Solar Mandate, and dragon-worshipping island clans follow
the local Lore Reference. Any culture/religion condition mentioned narratively
is a design rule to map to real mod identifiers later, not an invented game tag.

Every event must require its immediate premise: a protected target, damaged
settlement, stolen relic, productive works, or relevant rite must make sense for
the current province/owner. If it does not, choose another eligible concept or
postpone the encounter. A neutral host must not be punished for a hostile act it
has never committed. Text must travel: replace a coastal landing with a ferry or
supply crossing where the concept permits; otherwise gate that concept to coast.
Never pretend an inland province has a harbor or every owner has human peasants.

MOVEMENT AFTER EVERY EVENT

1. Before offering an encounter, identify other existing countries sharing an
   actual land or strait border with the CURRENT owner. Pick the destination
   country uniformly from eligible countries, not in proportion to province count.
2. Pick one eligible, owned land province of that country on the shared border.
   Exclude unowned land, impassable terrain, sea provinces, and a province already
   containing a different traveling adventurer modifier. The selected destination
   must belong to a different country from the source at the moment of transfer.
3. Resolve the chosen option for the source province/current owner, then remove
   the source presence and transfer that single adventurer to the destination.
   The small passive bonus leaves with the modifier. Permanent development and
   immediate country effects already awarded remain with their recipients.
4. All choices transfer the adventurer, including 'do nothing', paying them,
   helping them, or hindering them. No option purchases permanent residence,
   destroys the character, creates a duplicate, or redirects them to a chosen foe.
5. Island fallback, proposed explicitly for this design: if no eligible land or
   strait neighbor exists, treat another coastal country sharing a sea zone as
   adjacent for travel, and use an eligible coastal destination there. This is
   a maritime extension of adjacency, not unlimited teleportation.
6. If no destination exists even with that fallback, do not fire an encounter
   which cannot complete its required move. Keep the presence waiting and retry
   eligibility later. Do not award repeated rewards while it is stranded.
7. If ownership changes while an event is pending, revalidate the source owner,
   category, and destination. Cancel a stale encounter rather than rewarding the
   wrong government or leaving two copies. Only one event may be pending per
   adventurer. A country's annexation transfers custody with the province; it
   does not create another adventurer or silently delete the unique presence.
8. The no-two-in-one-country rule applies to the STARTING distribution. Later,
   different adventurers may enter different provinces of the same country.
   Each province holds at most one active traveling adventurer at a time.

PACE AND REPEAT CONTROL (PROPOSED, NOT SCRIPTED)

Aim for one encounter per adventurer roughly every four years, with at least
24 months between that adventurer's encounters and a six-month owner-wide
cooldown for this event family. Apply the cooldown from the previous resolution,
not afresh after every border change. These are initial pacing assumptions.
Do not repeat that adventurer's immediately previous concept when another is
eligible. Prefer unseen concepts within the applicable relationship category;
never use an Enemy concept merely because Friendly concepts have been exhausted.

Limit permanent development rewards from this system to one per owner every
five years and one per province every ten years. When a development reward is
ineligible, replace its +1 development component with +25 relevant monarch power
(same provisional WV +0.5), preserving any stated cost. This prevents circuit
farming without making movement stall. Exact pacing should be tested in play.

PASSIVE PROVINCE BONUSES

Each named presence supplies exactly one small LOCAL bonus while active. The
six suggested families are +5% local defensiveness, manpower, production
efficiency, trade power, tax, or institution spread. They never mean national
military strength, a free permanent idea, or a stacking development discount.

For this concept draft the small passive applies regardless of category: a
hostile visitor can incidentally stimulate watchfulness, trade, investigation,
or repair activity while still causing much greater trouble through an Enemy
event. This preserves the requested positive province modifier rather than
silently replacing it with a hidden permanent malus. The explanation should
match the family; it does not mean the traveler endorses the government.

BALANCE BASIS AND LIMITATIONS

Reference: Dev Stuff/Examples/Balance Guide.txt. This draft uses its Weighing
Value (WV) as the budgeting basis. Every Friendly/Neutral concept has intended
WV +1; every Enemy concept has intended WV -1. Every option prints its net WV
and lies within one point of its event's intended value. There are two to four
options per event; no chance-based results or hidden costs are implied.

Direct effect scaling used here:
- +1 base tax, production, or manpower = WV +0.5 (guide: +2 = +1).
- +1 percentage point army professionalism = +0.5 (guide: +2 points = +1).
- +0.5 innovativeness = +0.5 (guide: +1 = +1).
- +/-10 monarch power = +/-0.2 (guide: +50 = +1; losses use symmetric scaling).
- +/-0.10 years of owner income = +/-0.2 (guide: +0.5 years = +1).
- +5 prestige with the mod's prestige/monarch-power overflow behavior = +0.2.
  -5 prestige uses symmetric scaling at -0.2. The guide lists +25 = +1.
- +5 province devastation = -0.2 (guide: +25 = -1).
- One small rebel uprising at guide size 1 = -2. The guide explicitly prices
  noble rebels. Any culturally appropriate replacement type remains provisional
  at that budget until its actual strength and valid local conditions are checked.
- Doing nothing where no harmful outcome is described = 0.0.

A two-part development option normally costs 0.10 income-years, giving net
WV +0.3. Fame does not multiply these values. Enemy mitigation costs are small;
ignoring violence can instead produce devastation or, in the few suitable
uprising concepts, rebels. Most concepts affect only the current province and
its owner. None grants a country modifier, stability swing, free technology,
permanent national bonus, or diplomatic action against an unspecified country.

IMPORTANT: the guide mainly prices national modifiers and does NOT explicitly
price these six local passive bonuses. Their +5% magnitudes are provisional
minor-bonus suggestions, not claims of a validated WV conversion. They must be
balanced separately before implementation. Similarly, symmetric negative
scaling and non-noble rebel substitutions above are explicit design assumptions.
No unsupported modifier is presented as already approved or coded.

At caps or when an optional mechanic is unavailable, replace +0.5 innovation
with +25 administrative power and +1 point professionalism with +25 military
power (each WV +0.5). Use the established prestige overflow helper. If a cost
cannot be paid, do not offer that option unless its stated substitute is priced
and visible; ensure at least two valid choices remain, otherwise defer the
encounter. Resolve any change in the number of choices without silently giving
free rewards. Never delete development, start a war, convert the province, or
kill a ruler as an unstated consequence.

In later scripted localisation, expand each premise into the required two or
three paragraphs, choose a defined event picture, and give each option its
brief intent comment and WV comment. This file deliberately remains a concept
catalogue, not final localisation or an executable event file.
