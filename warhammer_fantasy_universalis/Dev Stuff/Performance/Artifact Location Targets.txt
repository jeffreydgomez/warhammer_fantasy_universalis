ARTIFACT LOCATION TARGETS — 24 OCTOBER 2026

Purpose

Replace the 24 capture allow world-province scans and the Destroy Nagash allow
world-province scan with saved locations. The supplied profiling capture attributed
about 84.6% of summed top-level time to these 25 allows. This is a priority signal,
not a measured speedup for this change or a claim about total game time.

Lifecycle

- events/War_Artifact_Setup.txt registers war_artifact_setup.1 on startup, with
  fire_only_once = yes as used by War_Adventurer_Setup.txt. Its immediate effect
  war_artifact_rebuild_locations reads the current province modifiers in one pass.
  It does not seed history positions, spawn artifacts, or move any modifiers.
- The new war_artifact_location_* names and war_artifact_nagash_holder ignore the
  former war_location_of_* targets and war_artifact_locations_initialized_v1 flag.
  Existing campaigns therefore initialize from their actual current artifact sites.
- Every live addition of an artifact modifier saves its province target immediately
  afterward: 70 decision placements, 46 diplomatic placements, Grail discovery, and
  the Dwarf Gromril Crown mission. Ordinary conquest/occupation changes the owner or
  controller of the same province; it does not require a new saved location.
- Five artifacts are consumed by Nagash's resurrection and clear their targets.
  His country is saved when the modifier is added and recovered during initialization
  for existing saves. Destroy Nagash checks that country still exists and still has
  the modifier, then checks its current capital. Destruction clears the holder target.
- Capture allows repeat their existing peaceful-AI gate and any existing resurrection
  gate before the target check. The target province must still contain the right
  modifier and be controlled by ROOT; the taker's capital must also be controlled.

Scope deliberately preserved

Potential/visibility, neighbor and war-enemy eligibility, already-owned rejection,
mission objectives, highlights, diplomacy eligibility, transfer rewards and costs,
relocation eligibility, and one-time modifier-removal searches retain their previous
rules. Targets are not used to determine decision visibility. No any_controlled_province
or has_saved_event_target trigger wrappers are introduced.

The system assumes each artifact has one location, as the existing design intends.
Starting histories are checked for duplicates. Console/save edits that duplicate an
artifact or move its modifier without using a scripted placement are outside the
tracked lifecycle. The rebuild effect can be used for diagnosis, but is not polled
or called in recurring decision triggers. Do not add a world-scan fallback to allow.

Validation

python3 "Dev Stuff/Performance/validate_artifact_locations.py"
python3 "Dev Stuff/Performance/validate_performance.py"

The suite compares preserved gameplay ASTs with 85be098c, audits all active placements,
and executes representative scripts in a restricted model. It covers missing/stale
targets, old cache remnants, actual relocated and unowned sites, 256 Fellblade states,
all capture/diplomatic paths, relocation with/without sieges, Grail creation, and
Nagash consumption and capital changes. It does not execute the EU4 engine.

Required engine smoke test after restarting EU4

1. Load a copy of an existing Grey Seer save with the Fellblade. Confirm the capture
   decision stays absent, including before unpausing. Check error.log for new errors.
2. Use a non-Skaven neighbor of a Fellblade holder. Confirm the decision appears while
   at peace but is unavailable without occupation; a non-neighbor war enemy should
   also qualify for visibility.
3. Occupy the actual Fellblade province while retaining control of the home capital.
   Confirm allow succeeds; third-party occupation or loss of the home capital must
   fail. Confirm the decision tooltip reports the correct province and conditions.
4. Capture, gift/request and relocate an artifact. Verify the target follows immediately
   and ownership/neighbor visibility remains correct. Save, exit and reload; repeat
   occupation and capture checks to verify target persistence.
5. Test a campaign where the Grail is absent, then discover it; forge the Gromril Crown.
   Both creations must become capturable when their actual provinces are occupied.
6. Resurrect Nagash, confirm the five consumed artifacts cannot be taken, then move
   his capital and test Destroy Nagash against the new capital. Also test initialization
   from an older save in which Nagash is already resurrected.
7. Profile the same save and the same elapsed game interval as the baseline. Confirm
   the 25 allows no longer contain any_province and compare total elapsed timings.
