SAFE CLEANUP REVIEW — 23 OCTOBER 2026

Scope: active WFU scripts. This is a static review for normal WFU starts under the
mod's intended exclusion of vanilla religions/cultures except animism. It is not
an engine playtest or a universal guarantee for custom nations or edited saves.
No gameplay files were changed by this review.

safe_cleanup_definitions.csv: 477 complete definitions suitable for coordinated
removal. Remove their pulse registrations, reform lists, estate privilege/agenda
lists and other obsolete callers together. Keep empty override files as needed;
deleting an overriding file can expose vanilla content. The CSV gives exact IDs
and current source lines, including which duplicate chinese_monarchy block is meant.

safe_cleanup_conditions.csv: 393 exact checks to simplify with their enclosing
logic preserved: 356 disabled-estate checks and 37 unavailable-tag checks.
These are not instructions to delete each predicate in isolation. For example,
an impossible OR alternative can be removed, but removing an impossible required
AND condition could enable unwanted content. Keep shared trigger/effect definitions
while callers remain. Some locations overlap the complete-definition list.

Nine gameplay estates are explicitly disabled by always=no: Brahmins, Dhimmi,
Ghulams, Jains, Janissaries, Maratha, Qizilbash, Rajputs and Vaisyas. Keep their estate
ID definitions while shared scripts reference them. estate_special is a required
modifier-support dummy and must remain. Cossacks, Eunuchs, Mages and other WFU
estates are active and are not whole-system removal candidates.

Monuments: only three literal missing project IDs were found in the reviewed
has_great_project checks: forbidden_city, porcelain_tower_nanjing and
monument_to_the_old_gods. Parameter $type$ and wildcard any are valid and excluded.
The last makes war_monument_events.89 unreachable and is included in the CSV.
The two eunuch agendas are separate repair/disable candidates, not unconditional
safe removals: Forbidden City's Leviathan selection branch is contradictory, but
its non-Leviathan Emperor-of-China branch can pass; Porcelain Tower can be offered
with Leviathan despite its missing target, and both have agenda auto-completion
paths. Do not delete the whole Eunuchs system.

Retain/repair rather than blindly remove:
- A37 has a national-idea trigger but no active tag registration.
- Z29 is a registered WFU country with an intro and ideas, but no province/core or
  explicit spawn path was found. Its history still assigns sunni and songhai.
- PAP has a Catholic history and a gated restoration agenda. It is not classified
  as an impossible tag; religion-dependent removals assume the intended WFU setup.
- sinner_personality is used by WFU personality save/restore effects.
- chinese_monarchy occurs twice; remove only the east_asian block at line 963,
  preserving the Cathayan block at line 1500.
- Breton in ruler_is_human and reformed in the Slaanesh center logic are corrections
  inside active Warhammer mechanics, not reasons to remove those definitions.
- Existing always=no content unrelated to this audit was not added merely because
  it is disabled. Province events, EVENT_PROCESS_OFFSET and Tributary military
  support have not been changed.
