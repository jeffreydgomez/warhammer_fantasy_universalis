OREON'S JUNGLE GLADE - HEART OF THE JUNGLE

Location and mechanical source
- Project id: oreons_jungle_glade; province 4880, Heart of the Jungle.
- Mechanically clones the current city_of_glass in
  common/great_projects/01_old_world_monuments.txt, as requested on 2026-10-23.
- The current City of Glass values are twice its original Karak Eight Peaks
  package. Clone the current script, not the older chat table or design notes.
- Starts at tier 1; cannot move. Construction cost 1000; initial time 0 months.
- Tier upgrade costs: 0 / 1000 / 2500 / 5000 ducats.
- Tier upgrade times: 0 / 120 / 240 / 480 months.
- Building, use, and upgrades require the owner to have elven_administration
  OR beastfolk_administration. Military administration alone is insufficient.
- Only area_modifier rewards; no national, regional, conditional, or upgrade
  effects. The site survives ineligible ownership but grants no active bonus.
- heart_of_the_jungle_area contains 4880 (Heart of the Jungle), 4881 (Core of
  the Jungle), 4882 (Jungle Depths), and 4883 (Lush Hollow).
- Province history, ownership, and the existing City of Glass are unchanged.

Lore
- Consulted Dev Stuff/Examples/Lore Reference.txt; preserve local mod canon.
- Name and flavor follow Z83 ideas: Oreon's Jungle Glade, enchanted bow staves,
  hidden canopy paths, and spirit-fruit groves, in war_ideas_l_english.yml.
- war_unique_intro_events.494 establishes a distant Asrai jungle enclave,
  watch platforms, waystones, and pressure from outsiders in the Southlands.
- Canonical setting reference: Bowmen of Oreon and their jungle sanctuary.
  https://whfb.lexicanum.com/wiki/Bowmen_of_Oreon
  https://totalwarwarhammer.fandom.com/wiki/Heart_of_the_Jungle
- The monument design expands the mod's existing glade, without claiming it
  is a separately named canonical landmark. Beastfolk use is the requested
  gameplay permission, flavored as coercion/profanation of the sanctuary.

Art

- Exact prompt saved in generation_prompt.txt; original art in
  oreons_jungle_glade_source.png.
- Final portrait uses the actual Dev Stuff/Image Reference/Monument_Image_Template.png

  gradient. No generated imitation of the frame.

  300 x 150 canvas, then the untouched template is composited at (0, 0).

  uncompressed 32-bit RGBA, with a matching PNG preview kept here.
